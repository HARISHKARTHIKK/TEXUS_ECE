Our Legacy

About SRM IST

SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Ramapuram Campus is a premier academic institution in Chennai known for engineering excellence, innovation, research, and industry collaboration. It nurtures 25,000+ students through industry-aligned curriculum and holds an A++ NAAC accreditation with strong placement records and entrepreneurial culture.

The Flagship Fest

About TEXUS

TEXUS is the annual flagship technical fest of SRM IST Ramapuram, bringing students from diverse institutions together to celebrate technology, innovation, and engineering excellence. It features competitions, hackathons, and workshops promoting problem-solving and real-world engineering applications.

Main Event

The ECO-TRONICS Hackathon

ECO-TRONICS is a national-level hackathon competition under TEXUS, focused on electronics, sustainability, innovation, and real-world tech problem solving. It challenge young minds to bridge the gap between complex hardware systems and eco-friendly solutions.
